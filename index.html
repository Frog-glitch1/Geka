GEK

Survive on planet Gaca

You're a gecko stranded on an endless alien world. Nothing stops you walking forever, and nothing out there is friendly.

  • Survive: lick glowmoths and glowberries, drink at lakes, find shade at midday and orange vents at night.
  • Fight: chain a 3-hit bite combo, roll through attacks, and watch for the red wind-up before a Skrel strikes. Kills drop meat and shards.
  • Shop: spend shards at the market (when no enemy is close) on supplies, permanent upgrades and skins.
  • Play together: use the same world code as friends to share one world. Duels are optional.
